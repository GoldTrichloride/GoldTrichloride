🗣️ Spoken pronunciation trick: Selenium (Se) & Tin (Sn)
These two share the same Mandarin pronunciation xī, which causes confusion in verbal communication.
My personal convention for spoken Chinese:

- Selenium: 非金属硒 (nonmetal selenium)
- Tin: 金属锡 (metal tin)

Note: This ambiguity is unique to Chinese. In English, selenium and tin sound distinct, so no such workaround is needed.

📝 Spelling trap: Phosphorus vs Phosphorous acid
These two look nearly identical and are easy to mix up:

- **phosphorus**: noun, the element phosphorus (P)
- **phosphorous**: adjective, meaning phosphorous; `phosphorous acid` = H₃PO₃

Mnemonic:

- -us: noun for the element
- -ous: adjective, for the lower‑valence oxyacid (-ous acid)
Compare: phosphoric acid (higher valence, phosphoric = 磷酸)
