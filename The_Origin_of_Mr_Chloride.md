# 小破事之我的理想装扮
# A Trivial Matter: My Ideal Outfit

（2026年8月14日）多邻国的广告都打到我玩的抖音小程序版的时尚百货城去了，我每次拿看广告的奖励时这不是弹时尚百货城自己的广告就是弹多邻国的广告，这是逼着我去试：我在课程中选择对我最中用的国际象棋，经过一番试用，结果是：我想不到多邻国无意中替我做好了我初中时的理想学习方法的“属性加成”，加上多儿的美点与我的看点有至少60%契合，然后，我的潜意识竟然对偶然在抖音刷到的多邻国官方发布的联名产品视频中看重了袜子，肾上腺素压不下去【我的理想专属衣物装扮中正好缺少袜子，这刚好可以成为其中的袜子，尽管眼睛看不着，但多邻国替我实现理想的心理作用会让脚即使泡在-273.15℃的液氦（常压下没有固氦）里也会觉得暖暖的（尽管在这个温度下脚和袜子都会被冻碎）】，但理智反对（袜子不属于装扮的看点，加上我不太会洗所有衣物，初中住宿时都是过水之后就直接晾，高中住宿时更是没时间换洗，同一整套衣服从周日晚上穿到周五下午，其中脚臭味发酵了上百个小时，在后期不穿鞋或没盖被子时清晰可闻，把“硫单质的淡黄和氯单质的浓绿配色的小家伙的图案的袜子”这样对待简直是暴殄天物），于是这对矛盾在我体内打架。

> (August 14, 2026) Duolingo's ads have even reached the mini-program version of "Fashion Department Store" that I play on Douyin. Every time I claim my ad rewards, it's either the store's own ad or Duolingo's ad. This practically forced me to try it out: I chose chess, which is the most useful to me, in the courses. After trying it out, the result is: I didn't expect Duolingo to inadvertently provide the "attribute bonus" for my ideal learning method from middle school. Plus, Duo's aesthetics match my preferences by at least 60%. Then, my subconscious unexpectedly fixated on the socks in an official Duolingo collaboration product video I stumbled upon on Douyin. My adrenaline couldn't be suppressed [My ideal exclusive outfit happens to be missing socks, and this could perfectly fill that role. Although invisible to the eyes, the psychological effect of Duolingo fulfilling my ideal will make my feet feel warm even if soaked in -273.15℃ liquid helium (there is no solid helium at normal pressure), even though at this temperature both my feet and the socks would be frozen and shattered]. However, my rationality opposes it (socks are not the focal point of the outfit, plus I'm not good at washing all my clothes. During middle school boarding, I just rinsed and hung them to dry; in high school boarding, I had even less time to change and wash, wearing the same outfit from Sunday night to Friday afternoon, with foot odor fermenting for over a hundred hours, clearly audible when I didn't wear shoes or cover myself with a blanket later on. Treating "socks with a pattern of the little guy in the light yellow of elemental sulfur and the dark green of elemental chlorine" like this would be a sheer waste of something precious). Thus, this contradiction is battling inside me.

至于我的理想穿搭的其他部分，那是追溯到2022年的事情了……和这张来自YouTube的Katuri TV的Katuri MV系列的第7集的视频封面的男孩一样，只是会在胸口那一块加一张“4 | AuCl₃”的名片；背面放上一个大大的浓黄色4，上方放上化学式代号和自己给自己取的名字（和运动员的衣服类似；原本的背面是空白的）；黑色短裤（除了在一侧加上一个白色的4，这也和运动员的衣服类似）和普通白色运动鞋（但我身为阿斯伯格人士，学也学不会系鞋带，这必须用转轮或魔术贴）不变，至于序号和代号的字体，采用这个号本来就在当标题用的210 Claytoy L，组成我在我的理想班级中的工作服：我是4号，化学式代号氯化金。考虑到这套衣服要么是为了拍主题曲MV而定制的，要么是洋货周边，就没有条件变现。

> As for the rest of my ideal outfit, that traces back to 2022... Exactly like the boy in the video cover of Episode 7 of the Katuri MV series from Katuri TV on YouTube, except I would add a "4 | AuCl₃" name card on the chest; On the back, put a large deep yellow 4, with the chemical formula code and the name I gave myself above it (similar to athletes' clothes; the original back was blank); Black shorts (except for a white 4 added on one side, also similar to athletes' clothes) and ordinary white sneakers remain unchanged (but as an Asperger's individual, I can't learn to tie shoelaces no matter what, so these must use dials or Velcro). As for the font for the number and code, use the "210 Claytoy L" font that I'm currently using as the title. This forms my work uniform in my ideal class: I am No. 4, chemical formula code Gold Trichloride. Considering that this outfit is either custom-made for a theme song MV or foreign merchandise, there's no way to monetize it.

我的序号和化学式代号的由来：首先，萌鸡小队中最著名的那位是黄色的老四，我也喜欢，因此当初我在平台注册用户时用户名写Katuri Chip，加上数字4是被谐音这个万恶的东西本身抹黑以至于很多人都避之不及的数字，但我觉得避开之后整个数字系统就断层了（这种厌恶感从我上幼儿园之前见到骏景花园的电梯按钮的1、2、3上面直接是5开始，发酵了很长时间），所以我心甘情愿地背上数字4作为自己的工号；金是黄色的，但太偏红，加上2023年时我偶然得知金可以在氯气中燃烧生成三氯化金，加上我把“成盐元素”听成了“成年元素”，认为化学式代号有氯是成熟的象征，我就征用了该化学式作为代号，就算后来我知道这是剧毒、强腐蚀性的物质，我也不打算改，因为这背负了历史象征，且剧毒、强腐蚀性物质不会因为剧毒、强腐蚀性就永远不会出现、永远没用。

> The origin of my number and chemical formula code: First, the most famous one in Katuri is the yellow fourth child, and I like him too. Therefore, when I registered a user account on the platform, I wrote the username Katuri Chip. Adding the number 4 is because this evil thing called homophony has tarnished it so much that many people avoid it, but I think avoiding it breaks the entire number system (this disgust fermented for a long time since I saw the elevator buttons in Junjing Garden directly jump from 1, 2, 3 to 5 before kindergarten). So, I am willing to bear the number 4 as my work number. Gold is yellow, but too reddish. Plus, in 2023, I accidentally learned that gold can burn in chlorine gas to form gold trichloride. Also, I misheard "salt-forming element" as "adult element", thinking that having chlorine in the chemical formula code is a symbol of maturity. So, I requisitioned this chemical formula as my code. Even though I later learned that this is a highly toxic and strongly corrosive substance, I don't plan to change it, because it carries historical symbolism, and highly toxic, strongly corrosive substances won't cease to exist or become useless just because they are highly toxic and corrosive.
