## 1. Nitric Acid Steamed Rice / 硝酸蒸饭

氯总妈进厨房蒸米饭，摸过米缸突然想整高科技，扭头翻出氯总藏的稀硝酸，哐哐倒电饭锅里：“都说科技改变生活，今天咱也试试酸煮米饭，保准香！”

> Mr. Chloride's Mom went into the kitchen to steam rice. After touching the rice jar, she suddenly had a whim to play with high-tech. She turned around and dug out the dilute nitric acid hidden by Mr. Chloride, pouring it into the rice cooker: "They say technology changes life. Today, let's try acid-boiled rice. It's guaranteed to be fragrant!"

结果按下开关没两分钟，电饭锅滋滋冒黄烟，塑料内胆直接融成黏糊糊的一坨，米粒子全炸成黑渣渣，厨房飘着一股呛得人眼泪直流的味。

> As a result, less than two minutes after pressing the switch, the rice cooker sizzled and emitted yellow smoke. The plastic inner pot melted directly into a sticky lump, the rice grains exploded into black cinders, and the kitchen was filled with a choking smell that made people's eyes water.

氯总从书房冲出来，瞅见这场景腿都软了：“妈啊你是想造硝化甘油还是想把咱家炸上天？硝酸煮米不是香，是直接给米饭办氧化火葬场啊！”

> Mr. Chloride rushed out from the study. Seeing this scene, his legs went weak: "Mom! Are you trying to make nitroglycerin or blow our house to the sky? Nitric acid boiled rice isn't fragrant; it's directly holding an oxidation crematorium for the rice!"

氯总妈还扒着锅沿懵：“咋还炸了？不是说酸能提味吗？”

> Mr. Chloride's Mom was still leaning on the edge of the pot, confused: "Why did it explode? Doesn't they say acid can enhance the flavor?"

氯总扶额狂喊：“这是硝酸啊！强氧化性王炸选手！别说煮米了，它能把米饭里的淀粉全烧碳化，再晚两步咱娘俩都得挂彩进医院！”

> Mr. Chloride slapped his forehead and screamed: "This is nitric acid! The ultimate trump card of strong oxidizing agents! Not to mention boiling rice, it can burn and carbonize all the starch in the rice. Two steps later, both of us would be injured and sent to the hospital!"

最后娘俩蹲在厨房门口，看着报废的电饭锅和一摊黑渣，氯总边擦眼泪边记仇：“明天就把你那破‘高科技做饭法’写进家庭黑名单，再敢动我的化学试剂，我直接把你那广场舞扇子泡进氢氧化钠里！”

> Finally, the mother and son squatted at the kitchen door, looking at the scrapped rice cooker and a puddle of black residue. Mr. Chloride wiped his tears and held a grudge: "Tomorrow I will write your crappy 'high-tech cooking method' into the family blacklist. If you dare to touch my chemical reagents again, I will directly soak your square dancing fan in sodium hydroxide!"

氯总从补习班冲回来，推开门看见这场景直接原地石化：“爸你疯了？这是硫酸啊！脱水性王炸选手，别说煮米了，它能把米饭里的水全抽干，再把淀粉直接碳化，你是想把咱家煮成火葬场还是想造硫酸亚铁炸弹？”

氯总扶着墙狂喊：“筋道个屁！硫酸能把电饭锅都腐蚀穿，再晚半分钟，锅漏了硫酸流出来，连地砖都得被烧出洞，咱爷俩直接给消防叔叔送业绩！”

最后爷俩蹲在厨房，看着漏底的电饭锅和一锅黑炭渣，氯总边抠手上的硫酸渍边放狠话：“明天就把你那破科普全删了，再敢动我的试剂，我直接把你那钓鱼竿泡进浓盐酸里，让你连鱼钩都剩不下！”

Mr. Chloride picked at the sulfuric acid stain on his hand and threatened: "Tomorrow, delete all your crappy science popularization. If you dare to touch my reagents again, I will soak your fishing rod in concentrated hydrochloric acid, leaving you not even a fishhook!"

He huffed and puffed and poured half a bottle into the rice pot, pressed the cook button and sat waiting for dinner.

没半分钟，电饭锅先滋滋冒白雾，金属内胆滋滋响着冒小泡，锅底直接被腐蚀出麻点，米泡在酸里全泡发烂糊，厨房飘着一股刺鼻子的氯化氢味，呛得老爷子直揉眼睛。

> In less than half a minute, the rice cooker sizzled and emitted white mist. The metal inner pot sizzled with small bubbles, and the bottom of the pot was directly corroded with pits. The rice was soaked and mushy in the acid. The kitchen smelled of pungent hydrogen chloride, making the old man rub his eyes.

氯总放学推门，闻着味当场跳脚：“爷！你咋把盐酸倒锅里了？这是强酸不是米醋啊！”

> Mr. Chloride pushed the door open after school. Smelling the odor, he jumped up: "Grandpa! Why did you pour hydrochloric acid into the pot? This is strong acid, not rice vinegar!"

老爷子还扒着锅沿懵：“咋不香还呛人？不是酸都能调味？”

氯总扶着门框狂喊：“盐酸是腐蚀王！能把铁锅蚀穿，把米泡烂，再煮两分钟锅漏了，酸流地上连瓷砖都得烧黄！你这不是煮米饭，是给咱家厨房做卤化腐蚀实验啊！”

> Mr. Chloride leaned against the door frame and screamed: "Hydrochloric acid is the king of corrosion! It can corrode through the iron pot and rot the rice. If boiled for another two minutes, the pot will leak, the acid will flow on the ground, and even the tiles will be burned yellow! You are not boiling rice, you are conducting a halogenation corrosion experiment in our kitchen!"

最后爷孙俩蹲厨房，看着糊烂的米和被蚀出坑的电饭锅，氯总边开窗散味边放狠话：“再敢动我试剂，我直接把你那老花镜泡进氨水，让你看啥都雾蒙蒙！”

> Finally, the grandfather and grandson squatted in the kitchen, looking at the mushy rice and the pitted rice cooker. Mr. Chloride opened the window to disperse the smell and threatened: "If you dare to touch my reagents again, I will directly soak your reading glasses in ammonia water, making everything you see foggy!"
